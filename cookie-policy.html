Last updated: May 17, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how WeddingClickz Photography ("WeddingClickz", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on the website weddingclickz.com. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to improve performance, and to provide information to site owners. Similar technologies such as pixels, local storage and tags serve comparable purposes.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

CategoryPurpose
Essential Required for the website to function and to keep it secure, including spam and abuse protection via Google reCAPTCHA on our enquiry forms.
Performance & Analytics Help us understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, time on site, referring sources) so we can improve content and usability.
Functional Remember your preferences and choices to provide an enhanced, more personal experience.
Third-Party / Embedded Set by embedded content and services such as social media feeds, video players and fonts, which may track usage according to their own policies.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third parties whose services we use, including Google (reCAPTCHA, Fonts and analytics) and social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest) when their content is embedded or linked. These third parties are responsible for their own cookies, and we recommend reviewing their respective privacy and cookie policies.

4. How to Manage Cookies

Most browsers let you view, manage, delete and block cookies. You can usually do this through your browser settings:

  • Adjust settings to refuse or delete cookies, in whole or by category;
  • Use private/incognito browsing modes;
  • Opt out of certain analytics and advertising cookies via the tools provided by the relevant third parties.

Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect the functionality of the website, including the ability to submit enquiry forms.

5. Consent

By continuing to use our website, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy, except for essential cookies which are necessary for the site to operate. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by adjusting your browser settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, law or our practices. The "Last updated" date above indicates the most recent revision.
