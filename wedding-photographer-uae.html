UAE Wedding Photography FAQs

How much does a wedding photographer cost in Dubai?+
Full-day wedding photography in Dubai typically starts around AED 6,000 for a single-event package with one senior photographer. Combined photo and 4K film coverage for a multi-day Indian or Arab wedding at a five-star venue usually falls between AED 15,000 and AED 45,000, depending on the number of events, crew size, drone coverage and album. We give you an itemised quote before you commit, with travel across the UAE included in most packages.
How far in advance should I book a wedding photographer in the UAE?+
Book 8 to 12 months ahead for peak UAE wedding season, which runs October to April. Popular dates at Atlantis, Emirates Palace or Madinat Jumeirah, and destination weddings in Ras Al Khaimah, often lock up a full year in advance. For summer dates, 3 to 4 months is usually enough.
Do you cover Emirati, Arab and Indian weddings in the UAE?+
Yes. Our Dubai team regularly photographs Muslim Nikah and Walima, Emirati and Arab celebrations, Hindu, Christian, Sikh, Jain, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayali, Marwari, Bengali and Punjabi weddings, and multicultural ceremonies. We know the ritual sequences in each tradition, so no key moment is missed, and we can provide female photographers for ladies-only functions.
Can you fly a drone at a wedding in Dubai?+
Yes, with the correct permissions. Drone flights in the UAE need clearance from the relevant civil aviation authority and from the venue, and large parts of Dubai near the airports and Downtown are restricted airspace. We handle permit applications where the location allows it, and we will tell you honestly at the quoting stage if aerial coverage is not possible at your venue.
Which UAE venues have you shot at?+
Among many others: Burj Al Arab, Atlantis The Palm, Armani Hotel Burj Khalifa, Madinat Jumeirah, Jumeirah Zabeel Saray, The Ritz-Carlton Dubai, Four Seasons Jumeirah, Bab Al Shams Desert Resort, Emirates Palace Abu Dhabi, Saadiyat Rotana and Waldorf Astoria Ras Al Khaimah.
Do you also shoot in Abu Dhabi and the northern Emirates?+
Yes. Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Al Ain are all covered from our Business Bay studio with no extra travel charge within the UAE. We also travel to Bahrain, Qatar, Oman, Saudi Arabia and India for destination weddings.
