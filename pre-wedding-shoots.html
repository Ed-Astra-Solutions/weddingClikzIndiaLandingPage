The Experience

Beautifully Unhurried

A pre-wedding shoot isn't about striking perfect poses. It's a few unhurried hours to laugh, hold hands and simply be together, while we quietly capture the way you look at each other.

"The main focus isn't actually the photographs… it's you."

There's a lovely side-effect, too: couples who do a pre-wedding shoot are far more relaxed in front of the camera on the wedding day itself, and it shows in every frame. Think of it as a rehearsal for feeling comfortable, not for posing.

Good to Know

Pre-Wedding Shoot FAQs

What is a pre-wedding shoot?+
A pre-wedding shoot is a relaxed couple photoshoot before your wedding day. It celebrates your relationship, gives you images for save-the-dates and invites, and helps you feel comfortable in front of the camera before the big day.
Where can we do our pre-wedding shoot in the UAE?+
Anywhere that means something to you: the golden dunes of the Dubai and Ras Al Khaimah desert, the Downtown Dubai and Marina skyline, Abu Dhabi's landmarks, the beaches of the Palm and Saadiyat, the heritage lanes of Al Fahidi, or a clean studio setup. We help you choose the backdrop that suits your story.
How long does a pre-wedding shoot take?+
Our City Shoot runs around two hours at one location. The Half-Day Shoot covers up to five hours across multiple locations and outfits, and destination shoots can run one to two days.
Should we do a pre-wedding shoot before booking the wedding photographer?+
It helps. Couples who do a pre-wedding shoot are noticeably more relaxed on the wedding day, which shows in the photos. It's also the best way to experience our team and style before the wedding itself.
Do you help with outfits, styling, locations and permits?+
Yes. We share a styling guide, colour suggestions and location scouting so everything works together on camera, and we advise on any permits needed for desert or landmark shoots in the UAE. Hair, makeup and outfit changes can be built into your package.
When do we receive our pre-wedding photos?+
Edited highlights arrive within one to two weeks, with the full retouched gallery, and an optional reel, shortly after.
