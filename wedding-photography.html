Wedding Photography FAQs

How far in advance should I book a wedding photographer in Dubai?+
For peak UAE wedding season (October to April) we recommend booking 8 to 12 months ahead. Popular dates and destination weddings often lock up a year in advance. For off-season dates, 3 to 4 months is usually enough.
Do you cover every wedding tradition in the UAE?+
Yes. Our Dubai-based team is experienced with Hindu, Muslim Nikah, Christian, Sikh, Jain, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayali, Marwari, Bengali and Punjabi weddings, and multicultural celebrations across the Emirates. We know the ritual sequences so we never miss a key moment, whether that's the pheras, mangalsutra, kanyadaan, sehrabandi or the saath pheras.
Do you offer both photography and cinematic films?+
Yes. Most couples choose a combined photo and film package so a single in-house team covers everything in one consistent visual style. You can also book photography only if you prefer.
Do you travel across the GCC and to India for destination weddings?+
Absolutely. From our Dubai studio we regularly shoot across the UAE, including Abu Dhabi, Ras Al Khaimah and the desert resorts, as well as Bahrain, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman and India. Travel and stay are added transparently to your quote, with no hidden costs.
When will we receive our wedding photos?+
You receive a curated teaser within 48 to 72 hours, edited highlights in 2 to 3 weeks, and the full gallery plus album within 6 to 8 weeks.
Where is WeddingClickz based?+
Our studio is in Bay Square, Business Bay, Dubai. We serve couples across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the wider UAE, with destination coverage across the GCC and India.
