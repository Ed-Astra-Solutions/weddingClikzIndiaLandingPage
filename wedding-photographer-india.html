An Indian Wedding Is Never One Day

It is the sehrabandi, the haldi, the mehendi, the sangeet, the muhurtham at an hour nobody wants to wake up for, and a reception that runs past midnight. Our studio at ClayWorks Shankaraa in Konanakunte, Bangalore, is built around that reality: multi-day crews, redundant gear and editors who understand what a family will actually want to look back on.

Every wedding is led by a senior photographer and cinematographer, never handed off to juniors. We carry backup cameras, lenses and storage to every function, and shoot to two cards so nothing is ever lost.

From the baraat on a Delhi street to the saptapadi in a Bangalore kalyana mantapa, the thaali at a Chennai muhurtham and a palace pheras in Udaipur, we know the rhythm of every tradition and stay one step ahead of the moment.

India Wedding Photography FAQs

How much does a wedding photographer cost in India?+
A single-day candid wedding photography package with one senior photographer typically starts around ₹60,000. Combined photo and 4K film coverage for a three to four function wedding usually falls between ₹1.5 lakh and ₹6 lakh, depending on city, crew size, drone coverage and album. Bangalore and Hyderabad sit at the lower end of that range; Mumbai, Delhi and destination weddings in Udaipur or Goa at the higher end. Every package is quoted itemised before you commit — see our full India cost guide.
How far in advance should I book a wedding photographer in India?+
Book 8 to 12 months ahead for the November to February peak season and for auspicious muhurtham dates, when good teams are booked out first. Destination weddings in Udaipur, Jaipur and Goa often lock in a year ahead. For monsoon and off-season dates, 3 to 4 months is usually enough.
Which Indian wedding traditions do you cover?+
All of them. Our team regularly shoots Hindu weddings across North and South Indian traditions — Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayali, Marwari, Bengali, Punjabi and Gujarati — as well as Christian, Sikh Anand Karaj, Jain and Muslim Nikah ceremonies. We know each ritual sequence, from sehrabandi and haldi to the muhurtham, kanyadaan, saptapadi and mangalsutra, so nothing important is missed.
Do you travel across India for destination weddings?+
Yes. From our Bangalore studio we regularly shoot in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Goa, Udaipur, Jaipur, Jodhpur, Kerala, Coorg and the hill stations, and we also travel to the UAE, Bahrain and Europe. Travel and stay are quoted transparently, with no hidden costs.
Do you shoot pre-wedding and post-wedding shoots?+
Yes. Pre-wedding and couple shoots can be booked standalone or bundled with wedding coverage at a lower combined rate. Popular locations include Nandi Hills and Lalbagh in Bangalore, the backwaters in Kerala, Goa's beaches, and the palaces and stepwells of Rajasthan. See our pre-wedding shoot page.
Can you fly a drone at an Indian wedding?+
Yes, in most locations. Drone coverage in India follows DGCA rules, and flights are restricted near airports, military zones and some heritage properties. We check your venue's airspace during planning and tell you up front if aerial coverage is possible.
When will we receive our wedding photos and film?+
A curated teaser reaches you within 48 to 72 hours, edited highlights in 2 to 3 weeks, and the full online gallery, cinematic 4K film and Italian leather album within 6 to 8 weeks.
